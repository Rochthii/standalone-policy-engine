# Software Engineering to ERP Career Roadmap

> **Decision:** Build depth as a Software Engineer through one real ERP authorization problem; use Odoo 17 for executable evidence and treat SAP as the later enterprise transfer target.

**Cơ chế ủy quyền ràng buộc giao dịch cho hành động của tác tử AI trong hệ thống ERP: Thiết kế và đánh giá trên Odoo 17**

*Transaction-Bound Authorization for AI-Agent Actions in ERP: Design and Evaluation on Odoo 17*

The agent is a delegated non-human software principal. The three thesis contributions are delegation-aware authorization, exact intent binding and commit-time enforcement. Current [bounded evidence](THESIS_SCOPE_AND_EVIDENCE_ALIGNMENT.md) includes the 75-post-test gate, separate runners, EVAL-02's 33 selected A/B/C outcomes and EVAL-03's separate Go/Odoo boundary distributions. These measurements do not establish causal approval overhead, production performance or a hiring guarantee through 2029.

## 1. Single path

```text
Software Engineering foundations
        -> secure distributed authorization service
        -> real Odoo ERP transaction integration
        -> graduation thesis with reproducible evidence
        -> ERP integration/security/backend role
        -> SAP BTP or ABAP integration specialization
```

This path avoids splitting the thesis into separate Odoo and SAP implementations. The transferable asset is the engineering capability: identity propagation, authorization, transaction integrity, approval workflow, observability, testing and deployment.

## 2. Graduation outcome

The thesis must demonstrate one complete Odoo 17 purchase-order confirmation path with:

- one-hop human-to-agent delegation;
- canonical business intent and material ERP-state binding;
- exact-action human approval and Separation of Duties;
- commit-time revalidation, replay protection and at-most-once committed effect;
- reproducible functional, adversarial and boundary-separated performance evidence.

The Go PDP is the implementation foundation. Low latency is supporting evidence, not the main research contribution.

## 3. Employability outcome

The repository should provide interview evidence for these Software Engineering capabilities:

| Capability | Project evidence |
|---|---|
| Backend engineering | Go services, gRPC/REST contracts, PostgreSQL and failure handling. |
| Security engineering | JWT, mTLS, delegated authority, least privilege, SoD and revocation. |
| Distributed systems | retries, idempotency, concurrency, policy synchronization and degraded modes. |
| ERP engineering | Odoo ORM transaction, purchase workflow, approval state and audit trail. |
| Quality engineering | unit/integration and bounded real Odoo/PostgreSQL evidence; current comparative benchmarks remain planned. |
| Operations | containerized testbed, readiness, metrics and bounded shutdown. |

Target entry roles: ERP backend/integration engineer, Odoo developer, enterprise application engineer, IAM/authorization engineer or backend engineer on business systems.

## 4. SAP transition after the thesis

Do not add SAP runtime implementation to the graduation critical path. After the Odoo thesis is stable:

1. Choose one SAP learning path (BTP extension/integration or ABAP) according to the target role and available environment; do not expand the thesis to cover both.
2. Map the verified concepts to SAP: principal propagation, OAuth/scopes and role collections, business-object authorization, workflow approval and transactional consistency.
3. Build one small SAP-side portfolio extension when an accessible SAP environment exists.
4. Claim portability only after that implementation has executable evidence.

## 5. Scope guardrails

- Odoo is the empirical platform; SAP is an applicability analysis and future specialization.
- Do not market the project as a replacement for native Odoo or SAP authorization.
- Do not claim general ERP validity from one purchase-order workflow.
- Do not spend thesis time on model training, multi-agent orchestration or unrelated benchmark competitions.
- Every major claim must link to a test, raw measurement or explicitly labeled limitation.
